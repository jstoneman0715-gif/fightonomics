Career arc: Islam Makhachev

From a Dagestani wrestling room to two UFC titles in two different divisions — mapped fight by fight.

Oct 2022 — UFC 280

Won the lightweight title

Submitted Charles Oliveira in the second round to claim the vacant UFC lightweight championship.

2023–2025 — The Reign

Four successful defenses

Defended the belt against the division's best, including finishes of Alexander Volkanovski and Dustin Poirier.

May 2025 — Moving Up

Vacated the lightweight belt

Walked away from the title at its peak to chase a second championship one weight class up.

Nov 2025 — UFC 322

Became a two-division champion

Outclassed Jack Della Maddalena over five rounds at Madison Square Garden to win the welterweight title.

Aug 2026 — UFC 330

First welterweight defense

Puts a 16-fight win streak on the line against Ian Machado Garry in Philadelphia.
